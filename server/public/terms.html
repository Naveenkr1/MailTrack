2. Description of the Service

Mail Track offers email tracking and delivery confirmation tools for users of Gmail and Google Workspace:

  • Read Receipts (Pixel Tracking): A 1x1 transparent tracking pixel embedded into outgoing emails to record when, how many times, and from what client device an email is opened.
  • Link Click Tracking: Optional link redirection to notify you when links inside your tracked messages are clicked.
  • Gmail Checkmarks Integration: Automated creation and assignment of Gmail thread labels ("✓" for delivered, "✓✓" for read) allowing check status visualization directly in official desktop and mobile Gmail applications.
  • Web Analytics Dashboard: Real-time reporting on delivery timestamps, recipient read activity, and historical telemetry.
