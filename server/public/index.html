Help & Support

Learn how Mail Track operates, troubleshooting tips, and how tracking beacons function.

How does email tracking work?

When you send an email with Mail Track, a transparent 1x1 tracking beacon image is securely embedded. When the recipient views the email, their email client fetches the beacon, registering a real-time read receipt.

Are self-opens counted?

No. Mail Track uses intelligent sender IP filtering, user-agent verification, and a 10-second grace window to automatically filter out your own sent preview reads.

How do link clicks track?

Tracked links route through your custom tracking redirector. When clicked, the redirector records the recipient and timestamp before instantaneously forwarding to the destination URL.

How do double checkmarks (✓✓) work?

A single check (✓) indicates the email has been sent and registered. A double check (✓✓) indicates the recipient has opened and read your email.
