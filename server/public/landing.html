Quick Start

Up and running in 30 seconds

No complicated setup or onboarding. It works directly inside your existing Gmail account.

1. Install the Extension

Add Mail Track to Chrome with one click. Connect your Google account securely using official OAuth 2.0 without sharing passwords.

2. Compose & Send as Usual

Open Gmail and click Compose. You'll see the Mail Track toggle active automatically. Write your email and send.

3. Watch Checkmarks Turn Green

The moment your recipient opens your email, your desktop chimes with an alert and the checkmarks in your Sent folder light up green.
